If you're here, you've probably already read my **About Me** page.

I'm a developer and a hobbyist robotics engineer. I've never particularly liked writing blogs—I barely managed to get an A in IGCSE English. We don't talk about high school. :)

But I'm more than my skills and the things I've learnt.

Outside of code, I have plenty of other interests. I'm a big gamer, a film enthusiast, and a music lover. I play piano and am working towards my ARSM diploma. I spend far too much time playing Minecraft, Ghost of Tsushima, and Jedi: Fallen Order, and I love anime, especially when it has deep world-building or strong emotional storytelling.

I'm also a huge Christopher Nolan fan. _Interstellar_, _Inception_, _Tenet_, _Oppenheimer_, and _Dunkirk_ are some of my favourites. I love the combination of big ideas, scale, and technical craft in his films, which probably explains why I'm so drawn to engineering-heavy and concept-rich work.

So, why the blog?

Honestly, I don't really have a strict answer.

I want somewhere to write about the things I find interesting. Sometimes that might be something I've built or learnt while working on a project. Other times it might be a film I watched, a game I played, an album I discovered, a piece of music I'm working on, or just some random thought I felt like putting into words.

It probably won't be particularly consistent, and it definitely won't always be technical.

That's kind of the point.

This is just a place to document the things I'm interested in, the things I'm learning, and occasionally the things I really like.

Welcome to the blog.
